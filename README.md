### Hi, I'm Chia-Yu (Millie) Liu 👋

I study how **hearing, speech, and language** relate to **cognitive ageing and early dementia**, using objective neurophysiological markers (EEG) and large-scale real-world clinical data.

- 🎓 M.S., Speech-Language Pathology, Asia University (Taiwan) · B.S., Psychology, National Tsing Hua University
- 🌎 Visiting Research Scholar, Communication Sciences and Disorders, University of Wisconsin–Madison (2023–2024)
- 🔬 Interests: resting-state & sleep EEG, auditory cognition, hearing loss and dementia risk, pharmacoepidemiology, meta-analysis
- 🌱 Currently: building a reproducible EEG pipeline for ageing and dementia while applying for PhD programs
- 📫 amy95031@gmail.com
